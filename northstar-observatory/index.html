01 · The galaxy

You are somewhere in here.

A barred spiral a hundred thousand light-years across. Our Sun sits two-thirds of the way out, in a quiet arm.

Light from the far side left before humans painted caves.
02 · One star

A small, steady sun.

Eight planets, a belt of rubble and a long tail of ice. The third orbit is the one that matters to us.

One astronomical unit: 150 million kilometres.
03 · Home

Clouds, oceans, city lights.

On the night side, the lights of eight billion people. Above them, a ring of satellites you can see from our roof.

On a clear night we track twenty satellites an hour by eye.
