Questions

Before you start.

Who builds my plan?

A licensed sleep clinician reviews your answers and builds the plan. The app helps you follow it; it never diagnoses on its own.

Do I need a wearable?

No. A sleep diary in the app is enough to start. If you already have a tracker, you can connect it.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Plans are monthly and you can cancel in the app.

Is this a replacement for a doctor?

No. If something needs in-person care, your clinician will tell you and help you find it.
