2000 to 2025

Slowly, then all at once.

Renewables held about a fifth of the world's electricity for a decade, then wind and solar changed the slope.

China

The biggest grid moved fastest.

China's renewable share doubled between 2010 and 2025, on the largest grid in the world.

Denmark

What all the way looks like.

Denmark now gets nine-tenths of its electricity from renewables, most of it wind. The world is on the same curve, just later.
