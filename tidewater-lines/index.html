Tidewater Lines · AEX service

Shanghai to Rotterdam, every Thursday.

One loop, eleven ships, the same day every week. Scroll to sail it.

Scroll to cast off
Day 0 · Shanghai

Loading at Yangshan.

Our ships berth at the deep-water terminal on the islands south of the city, loading around 14,000 containers in two days.

Day 6 · Singapore

Through the strait.

The busiest shipping lane in the world. We call at Singapore to swap boxes for Southeast Asia, then turn west into the Malacca Strait.

Day 19 · Suez

A canal one ship wide.

193 km of canal in a single convoy, about twelve hours from the Red Sea to the Mediterranean.

Day 32 · Rotterdam

Alongside at Maasvlakte.

Europe's largest port, built out into the North Sea. From the quay, rail and barge carry the boxes inland within a day.

Then it all happens again.
