One spec, 32 experiences.

Every page below is the same site.yaml (Halcyon), built by Scroll Studio with a different theme: { style: ... }. Each style is its own experience: a different scroll mechanic, layout and set of transitions, not a recolour. Open one, then use the arrows at the bottom (or your arrow keys) to flip through them all.

Anime introtheme: { style: anime-intro }Your scroll is the playhead of an anime opening. A silent cold open, a title card that smashes in, then hard cuts from shot to shot, freeze frames with vertical name cards, a VS split and an eyecatch, ending on the full-cast pose and a NEXT EPISODE card. Art decotheme: { style: art-deco }An opening night. Scroll parts gold-lacquered doors on the centre axis to reveal a spotlit headline under a fanning sunburst; every scene unfolds symmetrically from the centre, features are dealt in mirrored pairs, stats are billed on a marquee with chasing bulbs, an elevator dial sweeps floor by floor, and doors close and reopen between scenes until the ADMIT ONE invitation. ASCII arttheme: { style: ascii-art }A terminal session. Scrolling types each section's command at the prompt and prints the reply line by line (features as --help flags, numbers as neofetch, steps as a boot log, questions as a man page), with a live ASCII render of the product, a tmux status bar and keyboard shortcuts. Bauhaustheme: { style: bauhaus }One pinned poster that recomposes as you scroll. A red circle, a black bar, a yellow triangle and a blue square slide, rotate and re-dock into a new designed layout for every section; the circle that was the sun becomes the pie chart and finally the button. Blueprinttheme: { style: blueprint }One living drawing sheet. A plotter pen at constant speed drafts the product as you scroll (construction lines, outlines, dimensions whose values are the claims, leaders to a bill of materials) while the camera pans from view to view; the title block counts the sheets and the finale is an APPROVED stamp. Clay 3Dtheme: { style: clay-3d }Soft toys on a table. Everything has mass and squish; cards drop in and squash on landing, tiles wobble like jelly when you fling the page, a clay buddy tumbles and hops from tray to tray, and candy buttons push in under your finger. Comic booktheme: { style: comic-book }Read it like a comic. Each section is a page of panels, and every scroll moves your eye to the next panel. Captions slide in, balloons pop up in order, and the camera pulls back to show the whole page before you turn it. Flipbooktheme: { style: flipbook }A pad of animation paper. Every notch of the wheel releases one page, which curls up and over; each section is a run of pages whose pencil drawing animates as you thumb through and whose headline is lettered in by hand. Grab the corner to flip a page yourself. Glassmorphismtheme: { style: glassmorphism }Windows over a living world. Every word sits on frosted glass while a saturated colour world drifts behind at its own speed and changes hue section by section; a pinned glass lens holds still while the product turns beneath it, and feature layers fan apart in depth. Gradient meshtheme: { style: gradient-mesh }Weather. One slowly breathing colour field behind the page (a single WebGL canvas) that bookends the hero and the closing band, wakes in small patches on hover, ruffles when you scroll fast and quietly changes hue from chapter to chapter; around it, calm editorial whitespace. Nothing pins, snaps or cuts. Halftonetheme: { style: halftone }Reading a freshly printed paper. A front page with a masthead and a lead story in columns; pictures that resolve from coarse dots to a fine screen as they reach reading position, with a printer's loupe on hover; section fronts that print plate by plate in CMYK; classifieds, dot-field infographics, letters to the editor and a clip-out coupon. Holographictheme: { style: holographic }The information is in the angle. Every section is a rigid holographic plate that turns as you scroll past it; spectral light sweeps across its foil and a hidden mark appears at one narrow angle. Your pointer is the light source; features are a binder of trading cards; sections materialise behind a projector scan. Isometrictheme: { style: isometric }A fixed-angle drone over a tiny model world that builds itself. Every section is a district on one map; the camera glides diagonally between them while tiles drop in and buildings extrude, stats rise as towers, a token drives the road between steps, and the last district holds a keycap button. Kinetic typetheme: { style: kinetic-type }An instrument made of letters. Giant words inflate on their width and weight as they arrive and condense as they leave, lines skew and stretch with your scroll speed, marquees surge and reverse, and sections cut by colour inversion. Liquid morphtheme: { style: liquid-morph }Nothing cuts, everything becomes. One glossy blob morphs into each section's idea, stretches and wobbles with your scroll speed, divides into the stats and fuses through the features, while section edges arrive as moving liquid surfaces. Multiplanetheme: { style: multiplane }A stack of painted glass plates at real depths under one camera, and scrolling is the crank. Between sections the camera trucks in through the stack, near plates and the words pass the lens and the next stack emerges from the mist, with the focus pulled to the plate the words are on. Inside a list a foreground plate of trunks sweeps past at speed to change the words. The pointer slides the camera and rests to pull focus. Neo-brutalismtheme: { style: neo-brutalism }A desk you fill up and can mess with. Every scroll threshold slaps something down with a spring and it stays there, feature cards stack into a deck, marquee bands race with your scroll, stickers and windows can be grabbed and flung, and every button sinks into the page. Neon glowtheme: { style: neon-glow }The lights come on as you arrive. A walk down a night street where each section's headline is a tube sign, unlit glass until you reach it, then it stutters on and floods the wall with its colour while the one behind you dims. Features light in sequence, stats are segmented tube numerals, and the call to action is the OPEN sign. Paint on glasstheme: { style: paint-on-glass }One wet painting on a pane of lit glass, never cut. Scrolling is the painter's hand: the brush lays strokes from one scene's painting into the next, coarse first, each stroke dragging the old paint into the new colour, and scrolling back paints toward the earlier scene. Stop and the painting stops half reworked. Headlines are brushed in, numbers run, the pointer is a finger that smears the wet paint. Paper cut-outtheme: { style: paper-cut-out }Flat paper on an animation stand, cut out and pinned. Every section is a sheet held by a brass pin; scrolling swings the top sheet aside like a card on a pin, and the pieces on the sheets hang from their own pins and swing with the speed you scroll. A jointed paper arm points at what the scroll has reached, and you can take the hand and move it. Particlestheme: { style: particles }One handful of living sand. The same swarm of points re-forms into every section's subject (the headline, the phone, the numbers, the logo), migrating through a turbulent swirl as you scroll; the pointer is a hand in the sand. Pinscreentheme: { style: pinscreen }One lamp-lit board of pins holds the whole site, and every plate is a full pinscreen picture, deep blacks to soft greys, with sculpted numerals, the phone and your art modelled by the light. Scroll sends a wave through the board, a wall of pins that rolls out and leaves the next picture rippling behind it; headlines stay crisp in ivory over the pins, the mouse moves the lamp and pressing draws lines the board slowly pushes back. Pixel arttheme: { style: pixel-art }The site is a side-scrolling game. A title screen says PRESS START; each scroll notch walks a sprite through the level in whole pixels, features pop out of ? blocks as ITEM GET! cards, the testimonial is an NPC whose dialog advances a notch at a time, stats are an RPG status sheet and the call to action is COURSE CLEAR and a CONTINUE? menu. A READ AS PAGE switch shows it as a plain manual. Pop-up booktheme: { style: pop-up-book }A 90-degree stage pop-up book on a lamp-lit table. Scrolling turns the board pages, and the opening angle drives every paper mechanism, so each scene folds up out of the gutter on parallel folds while the one before folds flat behind the turning leaf. Pull-tabs raise the figures, a wheel turns the timeline, flaps lift for answers, and the button is the last tab. Retro VHStheme: { style: retro-vhs }A tape on a 4:3 TV. Scroll is the transport, so the on-screen display reads PLAY, PAUSE, SEARCH or REW with a running tape counter; every section arrives mistracked and locks, nav jumps are channel changes, and the call to action is ● REC. Sandtheme: { style: sand }One sand picture on a warm light table, never cut. Each scroll swings the side of a hand across it in an arm's arc, piling the old scene into a ridge and leaving the next behind it, words uncovered with the sand; a fingertip carves the headlines, a pinch of sand pours the numbers, and your pointer draws furrows that stay until the next sweep. Scratch filmtheme: { style: scratch-film }Drawn and scratched straight onto film stock (Len Lye, Norman McLaren, Brakhage's Mothlight). The screen is the gate and the page is a strip of film being pulled through it: the text steps through a perforation at a time and seats when you stop, a new scratched or dyed drawing is projected on every frame, the strip runs down the edge with its sprockets and a drawn soundtrack, sections are spliced, and the pointer is a needle that scratches the emulsion. A Hold switch stops the projector; Sound plays the drawn track. Smearstheme: { style: smears }Spider-Verse timing for a page. Everything holds on twos: the page is sampled twelve times a second, nothing glides, and in the one frame where a pose changes the headline, the cards and the buttons are drawn as a smear (stretched along the move, with colour plates trailing). Scroll faster and the smear becomes multiples; there is no blur anywhere, and out-of-focus things are the ones whose colour plates have slipped. Crossing into the next section while moving breaks the picture into one impact frame of flat colour and speed lines, and each section is a different dimension with its own print texture. Split-flaptheme: { style: split-flap }A station departures board. Scrolling advances the station clock; at each section the whole timetable clatters up one row, flap by flap, and the boarding row opens its detail at the information desk below. Stop-motiontheme: { style: stop-motion }A miniature set shot one exposure at a time. The page only moves while you scroll, and each notch of scroll is one frame on the animator's counter: set pieces are felt, card and clay, nudged into place in jumpy poses with their surfaces boiling, nothing moves between exposures, and between sections the set is struck and dressed again. Poke a puppet and it hops; switch on the onion skin to see the last frame. Wireframe 3Dtheme: { style: wireframe-3d }You fly through the blueprint. One world drawn only in lines, one continuous camera path from station to station; the copy is pinned to the model by leader lines, numbers are dimensions, the features fly apart as an exploded view with a parts list, and the line drawing solidifies into the product before the final assembly is approved for build. Zoetropetheme: { style: zoetrope }A prize wheel of cards on a strobe zoetrope. Every element of the page is a printed card with its own animated figure; spin the wheel and the figures come alive under the strobe, it slows with real inertia and lands on one card, and a click opens the card with its full content.
